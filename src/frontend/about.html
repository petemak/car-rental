About Us

Hospitality-grade service, at a price you can justify

We started Rwanda Roadways because too many first-time visitors to Rwanda were stuck choosing between a faceless international franchise and a phone-and-WhatsApp-only local operator. We wanted a real self-serve booking experience with a genuinely maintained fleet behind it.

Our standards

Before every trip

Every vehicle is inspected for mechanical condition, tyre wear, and cleanliness before it's handed over — inside and out.

Transparent policies

Mileage limits, fuel policy, minimum driver age, license requirements and security deposit are stated plainly on every vehicle listing — no fine print surprises.

Real local team

Our drivers and support staff are based in Kigali and know the roads, the parks, and the questions first-time visitors actually ask.

A note from our team

"We genuinely want you to enjoy exploring Rwanda — not just get from the airport to your hotel. That's why we built route guides alongside the booking flow, and why every car on our fleet is one we'd be comfortable putting a family member in."
